Why Choose Us
BUILT WITH PURPOSE,
DELIVERED WITH PRIDE
01
ISI Marked Premium Steel

100×50 bottom frame, 60×60 side stiffeners, 1mm GI/MS sheet paneling from JINDAL/JSW. Structurally engineered and ISI certified for durability.

02
50mm Glasswool Insulation

64 kg/m³ density glasswool — 50mm on roof, 25mm on walls — keeps interiors cool in Telangana's heat and reduces AC load significantly.

03
ISI Copper Wiring (L&T / Fenolex)

All electrical uses Fenolex/Polycab ISI copper wire. L&T MCB protection, separate AC point — fully concealed and safe for 240V single-phase supply.

04
Epoxy Paint Finish

2 coats epoxy primer + 2 coats synthetic epoxy paint on all surfaces. Anti-rust sloped roof, water-tight sealing, PVC vinyl carpet flooring on Birla Aracon board.

05
Factory Inspection + 7-Day Delivery

Every unit inspected and certified at our Gandimaisamma factory before dispatch. PAN India transportation arranged. 7 working days from order confirmation.
